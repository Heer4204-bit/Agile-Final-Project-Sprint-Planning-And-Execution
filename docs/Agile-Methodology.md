</p>📌 Introduction

Agile is an iterative and flexible approach to project management and software development. It focuses on continuous improvement, customer collaboration, teamwork, adaptability, and delivering value through smaller development cycles.

This document explains how Agile principles and the Scrum framework relate to the Agile Final Project – Sprint Planning & Execution.

# 🎯 Objectives
- Understand the fundamentals of Agile methodology.
- Learn the Scrum framework and its workflow.
- Encourage collaboration and communication.
- Manage changing requirements effectively.
- Deliver work through iterative development.
- Promote continuous feedback and improvement.

# 🧩 Agile Principles
The project demonstrates the following Agile principles:

1. Customer satisfaction through valuable delivery.
2. Welcoming changing requirements.
3. Delivering work in iterative cycles.
4. Encouraging collaboration and teamwork.
5. Maintaining continuous communication.
6. Focusing on working software.
7. Reviewing progress regularly.
8. Improving processes continuously.

# 🏃 Understanding Scrum
Scrum is an Agile framework that helps teams organize and manage complex work through structured iterations called Sprints.

Scrum Roles:
Role| Responsibility
Product Owner| Defines product goals and prioritizes the backlog
Scrum Master| Supports Scrum practices and removes obstacles
Developers| Plan and complete the selected work

Scrum Events:
Event| Purpose
Sprint| A fixed-length development cycle
Sprint Planning| Establish sprint goals and select work
Daily Scrum| Inspect progress and plan the next day
Sprint Review| Inspect the completed increment
Sprint Retrospective| Identify process improvements

# 🔄 Agile Workflow
1. Gather project requirements.
2. Create the Product Backlog.
3. Prioritize tasks and user stories.
4. Conduct Sprint Planning.
5. Execute selected tasks.
6. Review the completed work.
7. Conduct a Sprint Retrospective.
8. Improve the next iteration.

# 📊 Agile vs Traditional Development
Agile Approach| Traditional Approach
Iterative development| Sequential development
Flexible requirements| Requirements often fixed early
Continuous feedback| Feedback often occurs at milestones
Frequent delivery| Delivery often occurs at the end
Collaborative teamwork| More phase-oriented workflow

# 🌟 Benefits of Agile
- Better project visibility.
- Improved team collaboration.
- Flexible project execution.
- Faster feedback cycles.
- Continuous quality improvement.
- Better task prioritization.

# 📚 Conclusion
Agile and Scrum provide a structured yet flexible approach to managing projects. By applying these practices, this project demonstrates how teams can plan, execute, review, and improve their work through iterative development.
